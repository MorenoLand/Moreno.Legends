# Native player controls

GAME `0x800CB11C` acquires a ledge only while descending, with no active weapon-control flags and no horizontal collision correction greater than 32 raw units. The player bounds at `0x800DC800` are `[-32,32,-176,0,-32,32]`; acquisition also requires at least 64 raw units above the floor below. Two probes at facing minus/plus 512 angle units, radius `(32*13)>>3 = 52`, must return the same floor height strictly between 104 and 180 raw units above the feet. Acquisition snaps the feet 180 raw units below that floor, faces the collision edge and enters state twelve. The port uses its native collision bodies through Godot ray/slide queries for these checks; this is not a complete port of GAME `0x800B2934`'s box correction routine.

State twelve at GAME `0x800C7FA8` plays control29 (grab), control30 (hanging loop), then control31 when forward is held; pressing backward releases the edge. Control31's event1 changes the initial vertical speed -144 to -432 and forward speed -80; `0x800C71CC` applies gravity48 on subsequent climb updates. SLES `0x80041A54` shifts velocities twelve bits before adding them to 16.16 map positions, so one update moves `velocity/4096` world units. Ledge updates use the PAL 25Hz clock and play the already exported 30fps clips at 25/30 speed. Contact with the floor returns to the landing control; an ended climb without floor contact falls. This controller implementation has not been exercised in a runtime ledge probe.

The local player export reads `COMMON/PL00P000.BIN` for combat and `PL00P010.BIN` for the civilian model. The models use fifteen joints, with the original sixteenth source joint unused by these meshes. Packed positions and rotations follow SLES `0x8003F96C`/`0x8003FAD8`, including signed shifts and integer interpolation. Controls zero, one and two provide idle, run and walk.

## Buster

GAME `0x800CDCC0` selects controls 112/113 for weapon ID two. Control 113 applies during movement states two, seven and eight. Control 65 and cue `0x95` belong to weapon ID one's kick, not ranged firing. The Buster actor is GAME `0x800D03D0`; its emission uses cue `0x9A`, or `0xF4` underwater.

SLES `0x80040624` caches bone-seven translation at player `+0x4C8/+0x4CA/+0x4CC`. GAME `0x800D069C`–`0x800D06E4` reads this cache for projectile origin. The Godot attachment uses Bone_07's local origin. Upper-body mode two uses the root, head and bones five through seven; the base animation retains leg and inactive-arm world pose.

GAME `0x800CEFD0` binds the upper control at source record one, then sets spawn flag `0x20`. `0x800CE6F0` consumes that flag and allocates one class-one projectile. Markers four, nine and thirteen advance attack substates; they are not three projectile emissions. `0x800CF0E8` compares the sequential control pose against weapon-controller byte one plus the rapid stat delay. The normal base Buster uses pose four plus delay seven, giving an eleven-tick repeat interval on the port's 30-Hz animation timeline. The original PAL wall-clock conversion remains unverified.

The normal new-game initializer `0x800C35B0` clears Buster stats at `0x800C3688`–`0x800C36A8`, yielding attack level zero, damage eight, three maximum shots, fourteen native lifetime ticks, and rapid delay seven. `0x800B09D0` backs up existing stats before the temporary level-two preset; `0x800B0A8C` restores them. Buster stats are the part sums of the Equipment screen (SUBSCN `0x801DE3B4`, weapon two at player `+0x204`), each clamped to seven, and index the eight-level table at GAME `0x800DCCC0[2]` through `0x800CF3B8` (attack = damage and sprite level, halved when game byte `0x8009C7FE` is 4), `0x800CF40C` (energy = maximum shots in flight, counted at player `+0x19C`; `0x800CDC3C` blocks firing at the limit and `0x800D0B40` releases a slot), `0x800CF440` (range = shot lifetime in ticks, read by `0x800D0730`) and `0x800CF474` (rapid delay added to the firing record's fire pose, `0x800CE928`/`0x800CF0EC`). `tools/models.py` exports the tables to `assets/player/weapon_stats.json`; the port applies them in `scripts/player/player_controller.gd` (`_sync_buster`, `buster_ready`, `buster_damage`). Level zero reproduces the normal values (damage eight, three shots, fourteen ticks, eleven-tick interval); level seven gives damage 40, thirteen shots, 48 ticks and a four-tick interval. Not ported: the range of two for underwater shots (player `+0xB8` bits `0x88`, the same test that selects cue `0xF4`, `0x800D0728`), and the original equipment screen's third part slot (SUBSCN `0x801DBA20` sums three bytes at `+0x1A8`; the Equipment page and saves keep two). Levels zero and one use red `(255,32,32)`, two and three green `(32,192,32)`, four and five yellow `(160,160,32)`, and six and seven blue. GAME `0x800D0A04` draws the original 32×32 sprite at UV `(0,32)`, CLUT `0x7C92`, with normal and additive layers and a sixteen-segment glow. Native spin is `(frame<<7)&0xFFF`.

## Health and reactions

New-game health and maximum health are eighty; the life gauge scales with maximum health instead of showing the enlarged upgrade capacity at startup. Contact damage is scaled by the equipped armor (`assets/player/weapon_stats.json`, GAME `0x800CB678`): `max(1,(damage*(4-(armor>>1)))>>2)` where armor is item code minus `0x3BC` (player `+0x1A4`), so Padded 25 %, Link 50 %, Kevlar 75 %, and a strong reaction starts once the accumulated damage exceeds `16+4*(4-(armor>>1))`. Landing from a strong reaction without a helmet (`+0x1A2` = 0) costs 8 more health (`0x800C8E54`). Weak reactions use controls 32/33; strong reactions use 34/38, landing 35/39 and recovery 37/41. Knockback physics remain unported.

## Body parts

Equipment bytes live at player `+0x1A2` helmet (item code - `0x3B8`), `+0x1A3` shoes (code - `0x3C8`) and `+0x1A4` armor (code - `0x3BC`); `0` is empty. Wired in the port: armor damage scaling and knockdown threshold (above), and the helmetless landing damage (`0x800C8E54`). Traced but not ported: the normal armor and helmet knockdown guard (no read of the odd armor bit was found in GAME), and every shoe effect. Jet Skates / Hydrojets (`0x800CA268`, `0x800CAC0C`): skating starts with the pad mask at `+0x128` when shoes are 1 and `+0xBE` (water) is 0, or shoes are 2 and `+0xBE` is set. Asbestos (3, `0x800CE144`, `0x800CDFE0`): the hot/cold floor states in `+0xB8` bits `0x20`/`0x10` drain 2 or 1 health every tick, reduced to 1 every 8 or 16 ticks with the shoes; also `0x800B82B8` keeps `+0xBB` at `0x40` for every other shoe. Cleated (4, `0x800CD7D8`): with global byte `0x8007CEC0` clear, every other shoe sets `+0xBD` to 1 (the ice-slip state). Hover (5, `0x800B82E8`, `0x800CDB88`): prevents the numbness from electrified floors (flags `0x404`). The port has no floor hazards, ice or skating state to attach these to. The model set is selected by GAME `0x800BA6EC`: halfword table `0x800DBE10[helmet*6 + shoes]` holds the COMMON file index (9 = `PL00P000` ... `0x14` = `PL00P015`), so no helmet uses `PL00P010-015` and helmets one and two share `PL00P000-005`; shoes 1-5 map to columns `PL00P001`, `004`, `003`, `002`, `005` (and the `01x` counterparts). The variants differ only in the feet group (`0x1800`) and, without a helmet, the head group. `tools/models.py` `export_outfits` writes meshes plus `assets/player/outfits/outfits.json`; the port swaps the body mesh in `player_controller.gd` `_sync_outfit` and rebuilds the hose arm cut-out. An empty equipment slot therefore shows the helmetless head. Shoe effects and the underwater shot range have hooks only: `weapon_stats.body_part(equipment["body_parts"], "shoes")` returns the shoe value and `player.underwater` (shot range two, `assets/player/weapon_stats.json` `underwater_range`) is set by nothing yet because the port has no water, hot/cold/electric floor or ice states.

## Special weapons

Weapons 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 13, 14 and 16 run the common firing sequence GAME `0x800CE808` (record `0x800DC978`: start pose, fire pose, end pose, rapid gate, move flag): the module's control 96 plays from pose zero, the shot spawns at pose `firePose + rapid stat`, the sequence ends at `endPose`, and a held button restarts it immediately, so the repeat interval is `firePose + rapid - startPose + 1` ticks. The tank is player `+0x19A` (capacity `+0x194`, cost per shot `+0x198`, refill per tick `+0x196`, record `0x800DCA08`); the energy stat is the reserve `+0x192` (maximum `+0x190`, `0x7FFF` infinite) that refills draw from. `tools/models.py` `export_special_weapon(weapon)` writes `assets/player/weapons/weapon_XX.glb` (arm and animations through `export_weapon_arm`) and `weapon_XX.json`; `scripts/player/special_shot.gd` plays the sequence and `scripts/player/special_arm.gd` swaps the weapon arm in. Stat levels come from `native_context["native_special_levels"]`.

Spread Buster (weapon 14, module `PL00R0D`): `0x8010A104` spawns `2 + 2*(special > 0)` child bullets plus the firing actor, each yawed by `(index - 1 - (special > 0)) << 7` of 4096 per turn; range stat is the bullet lifetime, attack stat the damage, flight is the Buster's. The sprite is the 32x32 GAME page 0x0F texel block at UV (224,160) with CLUT `0x7C12`, drawn twice (colour `0xC0C0C0` normal, `0x606060` additive, tpage `0x4F`) at size 64 with a 45 degree spin step per frame. A hit spawns effect class `0x15` (`0x8010A8D4`): eight spark frames of `GP0 0x3A` gouraud quads. `tools/models.py` `NativeMachine` runs the original draw code unchanged in Unicorn with the projection calls replaced by a fixed centre and identity length scale and exports the packets (offsets in raw 1/256 units); `native_spark.gd` plays them additively. The `0xF0`/`0xF4` glow records the effect also emits (SLES `0x800393D8`: gouraud annuli drawn in screen space) are exported with the sparks, because `NativeMachine` also emulates the GTE subset (RTPS, RTPT, MVMVA, NCLIP, AVSZ4) and runs the SLES record pass `0x800390EC` over the effect's record list (`0x1F800050`), with a fixed camera of distance and projection distance 1024; every record renderer works in screen space, so the captured polygons are camera-facing offsets and `native_effect.gd` replays them with their blend mode (E1 tpage `abr`, semi-transparent bit, textured quads through exported GAME pages `effect_page_XX_CCCC.png`).

Homing Missile (weapon 6, module `PL00R05`): the missile starts at speed 256 raw, gains 64 per tick up to 1536 (two sub-steps per tick, 4096 raw per unit), lives `range stat` ticks, and steers toward the lock-on point through GAME `0x800CF1AC`: it arms after `4 - special` ticks, the turn accumulator gains `special + 1` per tick up to 64, the yaw rate is `accumulator >> 2` (doubled once armed) and the pitch rate twice that. Contact applies the attack stat (`0x800BFF20`) and the explosion (GAME class 7, run unchanged: size `(attack level << 4) + 0x80`, sound `0x8B`) damages actors inside `(size * (16 - counter)) >> 5` raw units with `4 + 2 * level`. The visible missile is the class 6 trail (GAME `0x800D2C10`): a 26-segment history of swept squares drawn as four additive gouraud wedges per segment (colour `0x34`/`0x36` fading 2 per segment, half sizes 8 for the head then `16 - remaining / 2`), reimplemented in `homing_missile.gd`.

Hyper Shell (weapon 5, module `PL00R04`): same rocket flight (`rocket.gd`) with speed 384 rising 64 per tick to 2560 over three sub-steps, a fixed-rate steering after an 8 tick delay (yaw 4, pitch 8 units per tick, only with a locked target) and a proximity fuse (it detonates once the distance to the target stops shrinking inside `0x400` raw units); the trail is the class 5 effect (GAME `0x800D287C`): the last eight positions drawn as pairs of textured sprite quads (a subtractive blue and an additive grey puff, frame and size by age), captured with `capture_sprite_trail`; the explosion is class 7 again with size `(level << 5) + 0xC0` and damage `(level << 3) + 8`. The `NativeMachine` record walker now also reads the in-place `0x2C` sprite records the SLES renderer rewrites into GPU packets.

Speed scale cross-check: the Buster's lock-on shots use speed `0x400` (GAME `0x800D0680`) and the port's 15 units per second equals `0x400 * 2 sub-steps * 30 / 4096`, which confirms 4096 raw per unit for `0x8004197C` movement. Unlocked Buster shots use `-0x400` too (`0x800D0594`), so 15 units per second is right for both; the Spread Buster's bullets use `-0x600` (`0x8010A214`), 22.5 units per second.

Machine Gun Arm (weapon 13, module `PL00R0C`, partly decoded, not ported): the bullet (`0x8010A118` spawn, `0x8010A4B4` flight) flies at speed `-0x600` (22.5 units per second, three sub-steps per tick, range stat as lifetime, weak steering with the target dropped past `0x200`), consumes the tank (`0x198` per shot), plays sound `0x109`, registers a short hit segment (`0x800427A0`) and spawns two helpers: a kind-1 class `0xA` actor (GAME `0x800D4A60`, a gun-side effect positioned from player yaw, sound `0x80`) and a class `0x11` ballistic shell casing (module `0x8010A8C4`, gravity `0x40` per tick, a `0x2C` sprite record at UV (192,160) 8x8 with CLUT `0x7D90`, bounce sound `0x10A`); the bullet itself has no draw routine in the module.

Ground Crawler (module `PL00R06`) registers only a blob shadow through `0x8003B268` (record list `0x1F800070`: floor offset, id `0x30`, size `0x100`) and its explosion; no other draw routine or model was found, so its visible body is unresolved and it is not ported.

Cutscene outfit swap: ST0B, ST1B and ST1B01 call GAME `0x800B09D0` once event flag `0x246` is set (setting `0x24B`), which stores helmet 1, shoes 0, armor 2 and Buster levels (2,0,0,2); ST55 calls `0x800B0A8C` when flag `0x249` or `0x680` is set and `0x24C` is clear (setting `0x248`). The port keeps the real equipment, applies the override virtually (`player.outfit_override`, saved as `native_outfit_override` in the game state) and reuses the model, armor and Buster paths.

Pad words: `+0x10E` is the held mask (pad struct `0x8009BEC0+0x28`), `+0x110` the newly pressed mask (`+0x2C`); `+0x11C`/`+0x11E` forward/back, `+0x120`/`+0x122` strafe left/right, `+0x124`/`+0x126` turn left/right, `+0x12C` jump (the assignment of the 120/124 pairs follows the turn/strafe use in `0x800C3F8C`..`0x800C41D0`). Padded Helmet roll: the state-0xE (weak hit) handler hook `0x800CA80C` calls `0x800CB008` each tick in substate 2 (from pose five of the hit clip) when the helmet byte `+0x1A2` is 2. With `+0x10E` it takes the jump bit (flag 3 when `+0x3C` is zero and heading `+0xF2` is zero), else exactly one of turn left/right held, else exactly one of strafe left/right held (left = flag 2, right = flag 3); it sets state 10 with `+0xB` = flag, resets the weapon controller (`0x800CCF80`) and waits. State 10 (`0x800C79F4`, substates 0-4): 0 waits for the hit clip to end with `+0xEC` = -1 (hits are ignored), then plays control 24 (flag 3) or 25 (flag 2) with sideways speed `+0x38` = -512 or +512 (substate 1; vertical and forward zero) and sound `0xA0`; 1 on clip end sets heading offset `+0xF2` = -0x400 or +0x400, `+0xEE` = 3 or 1, control 26, vertical `+0x3A` = -128 and forward `+0x3C` = -512 (a 512-speed leap to the side); 2 adds gravity 48 per tick (`0x800C71CC` mode 0) until the clip ends, then control 28 (airborne) or 27 (landed); 3 keeps gravity until contact, then control 27; 4 waits for event one of control 27 (sound `0x93`) and returns to state 8. No air steering (`0x800C7298`) is called in state 10. The port (`player_controller.gd` `_roll_window`, `_begin_roll`, `_update_roll`) holds a helmet-2 player in the weak reaction from pose five to the end of the clip (the original keeps everyone locked to the end), reads the held inputs there, and starts the roll at the clip end with the exact speeds, 25 Hz ticks and clips above; side = the pressed side of the model (original sign of the lateral axis not verified); horizontal speed is zeroed on touchdown and the heading offset is applied as a pure sideways move; air landing without roll end uses the normal falling phase.

Grab: `0x800CFF88(actor, reach, mode)` (called by ST41/ST42 class 97, callback `0x800EF2BC`, state table `0x801012C0`) fails while player `+0x50` is set; inside a box of ±reach (0x40) on all three axes it copies the actor position into the player, sets state 0x11 (substate 0 = control 91 intro, mode 0 = control 90 directly) and returns 1. State 0x11 (`0x800C91AC`) zeroes the speeds and `+0xEC` (the player stays hittable), plays control 91 (substate 0) then control 90; the actor advances the player substate when its 16-tick telegraph ends (`0x800EF55C`) and otherwise moves to its miss state (`0x800EF728`, which retries the grab each tick). The held state (`0x800EF7D4`) shakes the model, and at the grab clip end sets the mash counter to 0x20; every tick `0x800D008C` copies the actor position to the player and, when `(+0x11C|+0x11E|+0x124|+0x126|+0x12C) & +0x110` (any newly pressed forward, back, turn or jump bit) is set, decrements the counter; at zero it calls `0x800CCF80(player,0,0)`, clears `+0x2AC` and sets state 0 (no invulnerability is granted). While the counter runs, `0x80044070(player,-1)` drains one health every fourth tick while health is above zero (no armor scaling, never lethal). A player hit (states 0xE/0xF) releases the grab; after release the actor plays control 2, shrinks for 16 ticks and re-arms, so a player who stays inside the box is seized again. The port: `player.begin_hold(holder, profile)` pins the player to the holder at 25 Hz, plays clips 91/90 (`hold_grip`), `hold_arm()` starts the mash counter (`mash`, default 32) and the drain (`drain_every` 4, `drain` 1), `end_hold(escaped)` frees the player and `hold_ended(holder, escaped)` fires; `take_hit` and `reset_at` end a hold. `scripts/world/enemies/native_dungeon_grabber.gd` is the class 97 port (registered in `world.DUNGEON_ENEMIES` for ST41 and ST42; the class has no attribute table). Not ported: the sprite particles of the telegraph and grip (`0x8003E8F8`, `0x80042704`), the `+0x294` write while the grab marker is six or more, and the model ring from flag 2. Note: the Icefield spitter latch (`0x800CFABC`, `0x800CFB24`, `0x800CF998`) is the player lifting the enemy (state 0x10), not a grab of the player; the port already implements it as `begin_lift`/`carried_actor`. The outfit swap guards are `0x80078CC8` (byte 0 of the scene-state struct that GAME `0x800BDBC0`/`0x800BE24C` clear and `0x800BE330` starts scenes with; zero means no scene running) and `0x8007CEC0` (also tested by the Cleated shoes at `0x800CD7C4`; meaning unresolved); the port applies the swap without them.

## Death

SLES `0x80044070(actor, delta)` applies damage only while the game block `0x8009C7E8+1` is 2 and flag `0x6F4` is clear. From HP above zero, an overshoot clamps HP to 0 and returns 1; a hit at HP 0 or below (`0x80044020`) stores −1 and returns −1. Any nonzero return selects the strong reaction in `0x800CB678` (state 0xF, `0x800C8C44`), so HP 0 is alive and the next hit kills. Substate 3 recovers with controls 37/41 only when HP ≥ 0; at HP −1 it holds the landing control 35/39, slides to rest and `0x800C8E10` sets player `+8` = 2. `0x800CC724` then writes game block +0 = 8, +1 = 0. Map interaction `0x800C4364` runs only at HP ≥ 0. The ST1E timer routine `0x800E7B88` fails the mission at HP exactly 0 before a killing hit can land.

Mode 8 (`0x800B00D0`): `0x80048944(1)`; transition `0x14` (`0x12` when flag `0x246` is set) with `0x80020984(0x3F, 0xB6, 0)` fading music channels by 182 per update from 0x3FFF; after the fade `0x80020C90` and engine phase 5 reload `COMMON/DEMO.BIN` (state 6, `0x800ADE7C`). With flag `0x246` set the fade completes into `0x800B0B18` instead, restoring mode 3 in ST0B (flag `0x248` clear) or ST1B. The ST0B/ST1B handlers heal the player and clear `0x246`. No zenny or item changes were found, and there is no retry or continue prompt.

DEMO state 6 loads file 4 (`COMMON/G_OVER00.BIN`: GAME/OVER textures at VRAM words (640,256) and (768,256), CLUT row 496, sequence section `0x9000`, bank `0x9800`), clears the transition, starts music 0xC, raises a tint from 0 to 0x80 in steps of 2, then holds `(3−0x1F800005)<<8` updates. Start, Cross or Triangle (`0x5008`) skips it. Transition `0x20` then returns to DEMO state 0, the title. The background is the Gouraud quad colour `(0, 0, tint·14>>7)`; four 8-bit sprites at (72, 208) in 640×480 space carry the text. `G_OVER01.BIN` and `G_OVER02.BIN` hold Japanese-text artwork; no loader for file IDs 5 or 6 was found.

## Jumping

GAME `0x800C68BC` starts stationary control 16 before launch, then uses 17 for rising, 18 for falling and 19 for landing. Moving jumps use controls 20, 21 and 22. Their `0xFF` terminal control flags hold the final pose until the corresponding physics state changes; landing waits for its control to finish before returning to idle or locomotion.

The normal native launch velocity is −800, held-jump gravity adds 48 per update, and releasing jump brakes the ascent by 576. Native vertical displacement is `−velocity/4096` per update. The current 30-update gameplay adapter measures approximately 1.727 map units for a held jump, matching the native discrete ascent sum of 1.7265625 within the collision margin. Moving launch speed is 512 and airborne target speed is 640, with acceleration 16 per update. Underwater gravity remains unported.

## Yosyonke gates and ground contact

The ST08 landing-pad gate to ST0D uses contact type three at native `(1536, 0, -2864)`, placement 20, with the manual forward probe consumed by GAME `0x800B8588`. Its door resource is controller class two (`0x800D1B10`), with a fixed companion leaf. The runtime uses that contact and heading rather than rejecting it with an additional ray to a point behind the visible gate; the exported `native_door.source_panel` supplies the prompt and animated transition panel.

Placement 20 also supplies original kind-zero and kind-`0x1C` collision volumes extending above the rendered gate. Manual door placements must retain these volumes. Opening a transition door cuts only its recovered doorway aperture through the native boxes, and closing restores them. The ST08 gate placement is a closed slab whose front and top faces coincide with the two leaf models, while the recovered panel holds only its back face: opening also removes every placement face lying inside the closed leaves' bounds, and leaves a transition door's own models without camera collision, since the camera collides with the native boxes (a silent probe showed the fixed companion leaf stopping the camera 0.65 behind the player at height 0.61; it now stays 2.3 behind at height 1.03). ST08/ST09 collision metadata was compared directly with a fresh decode of the disc and matched. Silent Redot physics probes blocked all 29 ST08 and 50 ST09 tested tall boundary volumes at airborne height two; the closed landing gate blocked the player capsule at heights 0.1, 1.5 and three, while its opened aperture passed it. A rendered probe exercised the native lock dialogue and emitted the ST0D area-zero transition after opening.

The Godot movement adapter explicitly applies floor snap after a grounded walking slide, before selecting any fall state. It does not apply this during a jump, free flight or noclip. A probe using the actual player controller walked the Post Office stairs from height 0.50 to 1.00 and back without pressing Jump or entering the falling state. These probes verify collision and transition requests; they do not cover every town route or load the requested destination through the main menu host.

## Civilian equipment

SLES `0x80078ADC` safe descriptor row `[0,1,2,4,6]` selects native normal-hand groups `0x26F0` on bones 2–4 and `0x1DD0` on bones 5–7. The exporter uses these actual P010 vertices and its bare head; it does not mirror the combat glove or hide part of the Buster mesh. ## Icefield spitter lifting

ST11 class 23's `0x800E7C78` calls GAME `0x800CFABC(actor, 0)` when its private flags have neither `0x02` nor `0x40` set. This selects player lifting state `0x10` (`0x800C9020`, controls 72/74); the enemy does not grab the player. Spitter substate 3 (`0x800E82E4`) attaches it to the player's averaged hand positions through `0x800CFB24`, with control 9 for 120 actor updates, then control 10 for 20 updates. The actor is not registered for damage while held.

The escape branch `0x800E84FC` registers collision mode 2 and waits for `0x800CF998` to return 2: player hurt states `0x0E/0x0F`, or an airborne carrier. Its contact attack interrupts the lift before the actor moves with vertical −128, forward 128 and gravity 64; grounding leads to substate 4. Throwing uses `0x800E8320`/`0x800E844C`, pitch-transformed `(0, −256, −1024)` through SLES `0x800421E0`, gravity 64 and collision mode 1. An actor hit word containing `0x20000` selects airborne death substate 9; grounding without that hit returns to retreat substate 5. Native Unicorn probes verified the hold/escape timing and status contract, and a silent Redot probe exercised the real player and spitter resources through pickup, escape contact and throw.

## Friendly lock targets

SLES `0x80043570` accepts registered hit volumes through the byte table `0x8006B47C[(mask & 0xE00000) >> 21] & 0x40`; bit `0x80000` excludes a volume from primary tracking. ST08's normal NPC callback registers mask `0x400010` at `0x800E7A98`, and Roll's following callback registers `0x408010`/`0x408014` at `0x800E96F4`; these are eligible friendly targets. Exported `native_lock_on` profiles retain the callback, registration call, mask and recovered hit-point offset or first cached volume.

The initial search chooses the nearest eligible target with a projected X coordinate inside the original 320-pixel viewport, within `0x3400` native units, before its nearest-target fallback within `0xC80` units. It contains no enemy-versus-friendly priority. Original MIPS registration and selection probes confirmed this ordering, including a nearer visible friendly winning over a farther visible enemy; selection probes supplied projected viewport coordinates for the GTE operations. A silent rendered Redot probe selected the original ST08 NPC resource and displayed its reticle; hidden, inactive and primary-excluded registrations were rejected.

## Shop windows and instant warps

SLES `0x8004D060` reads the shared pad words at `0x8009BEC0+0x28/+0x2C` for page advancement without clearing them. Shop windows receive the same input snapshot each tick; a persistent greeting therefore cannot consume a confirmation intended for its adjacent choice window. Rendered input probes exercised selling an unequipped Power Raiser, excluding an equipped part, recharging both tanks and leaving all six ST1A/ST1B/ST25/ST29/ST3B/ST3F banks. These probes used the original banks with an ST08 gameplay host and explicit inventory/wallet fixtures, rather than entering each vendor through its town route.

Gravity modifier: the ST41/ST42 stage routine (0x800E7398) writes player `+0xBF` each tick (12 with event flag 0x155, -8 with 0x153, else 0). GAME `0x800C7250` adds `(3 * (16 + modifier)) >> 4` (3 when zero, 1 in water at zero) to the fall velocity per tick; positive modifiers also lower the run speed cap (`-0x400` to `-0x340` at 12, `0x800C62E0`..). The port scales its fall step the same way (`gravity_modifier`, `native_gravity.gd`); the speed cap and water halving are not ported. `+0xBD` is the ice-slip state (see docs/actor_coverage.md E0/25), not a release flag.
